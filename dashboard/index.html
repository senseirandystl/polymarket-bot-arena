Paper Balance

Shared virtual USDC pool for paper mode. All bots trade from this one balance against real Polymarket order books. Enter the balance you want and click Save — it tops the pool up to that amount (past P&L and open positions are preserved), so entering 200 makes the available balance $200.

Kelly Fraction

Fraction of the full Kelly-optimal bet size used for every trade (bet = fraction × edge/(1−price) × bankroll). 0.25 = quarter-Kelly (conservative), 1.0 = full Kelly (growth-optimal only if the model's probabilities are exact — aggressive otherwise). The arena picks up changes within seconds; no restart needed.
